How the blog works

Short, current, opinionated — in that order

These are not research papers and they are not marketing copy. They are short pieces — usually two screens, two pages at most — on what I am seeing in the field and what I think it means for knowledge. They are written in my voice, on my view of the latest news, and published in chronological order so the oldest thinking is at the bottom and the newest is at the top.
