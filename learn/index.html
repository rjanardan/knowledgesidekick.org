FAQ

Is any of this free?

Yes. Everything on this site is free to read — not-for-profit, no fee, no ads, no tracking. The courses are the one paid item, and Course terms states what stays free and what the licence on course material is.

Do I need a course to use the site?

No. Every page here stands on its own and nothing is gated. The courses exist for people who want the material taught in sequence rather than read.

What does the glossary cover?

The vocabulary the rest of the site assumes: data to wisdom, representation and formats, provenance and currentness, agents and protocols. Where a term has a published definition behind it, the glossary says which specification it comes from.

Sources

The glossary rests on published definitions rather than on this site's usage. The specifications behind those terms:

  1. SKOS Simple Knowledge Organization System Reference — W3C Recommendation. Concept schemes and thesaurus relations.
  2. PROV-O: The PROV Ontology — W3C Recommendation. Provenance.
  3. Model Context Protocol documentation — the tool and context interface.
  4. Open Knowledge Format (OKF) specification — a published concept-bundle format.
