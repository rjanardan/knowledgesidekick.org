FAQ

Are the courses free?

No. Courses are the one thing on this site you pay for. Every page on knowledgesidekick.org stays free to read, with no fee and no ads — that commitment is permanent.

Where do I pay and enrol?

On knowledgesidekick.com, the business side. Payment, invoicing and refund terms are set there, per course, at the point of enrolment.

Does buying a course change what is free here?

No. Nothing on this site is gated, and no course will be used to gate it. If you would rather build the practice yourself from the free pages, that path stays open and is not deliberately incomplete.

When do the first courses open?

None are open for enrolment yet. When one opens it will be listed on this page with its syllabus, its length, and the enrolment link on the .com side.
