Course terms

What is free, what is paid, the licence on course material, and where prices, payment and refunds are handled.

Courses · Prices and enrolment on .com

Direct answer

The reading is free, the teaching is paid

These terms cover courses only. They do not apply to anything else on this site, because nothing else is sold: the research, standards, adoption and learning pages are free to read, with no fee and no ads.

What is free and what is paid

Free, permanently: every page on knowledgesidekick.org, including the notes on open specifications and the ones on proprietary platforms. Paid: enrolment in a course, which buys a seat, materials and the instructor's time. The same subject matter appears in both — the course is the guided version, not the complete version. The free pages are meant to be enough to work from if you would rather not pay for guidance.

Where payment and refunds are handled

Prices, applicable taxes, invoicing, the delivery schedule and the refund terms are stated per course, on knowledgesidekick.com, before you pay. This site takes no payments, holds no payment details and offers no checkout.

The licence on course material

Course materials are licensed to the person who bought them, for their own work and study, and may be applied commercially. They may not be redistributed, resold or republished, and may not be used to deliver competing training. This is narrower than the pages on this site, which you are free to read, quote with attribution, and use in your own work.

If something goes wrong

Course administration — access, schedules, invoices, and anything about a payment — is handled at knowledgesidekick.com. For anything about the free material on this site, including a correction you think a page needs, use the contact page.

FAQ

What exactly is paid?

Courses, and only courses. Every page on knowledgesidekick.org is free to read. Nothing on this site is gated, and buying a course does not unlock anything here that is not already open.

Where are prices and refunds stated?

At the point of enrolment, per course. Prices, taxes, invoicing and refund terms are set there because that is where the transaction and the legal terms live.

What licence do I get on course material?

A personal, non-transferable licence to use the material for your own work and study. You may apply what you learn commercially. You may not redistribute, resell or republish the material, or use it to deliver competing training.

Who do I contact about a course?

Use the contact details published on knowledgesidekick.com, which handles all course administration, or the contact page here for anything about the free material.
